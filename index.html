Privacy Policy

PipDrop is designed with a strict privacy-first philosophy. The game operates 100% offline. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal information (such as your name, phone number, address, or contacts).

All your gameplay progress, high scores, unlocked dice skins, jar themes, and in-game settings are stored strictly locally on your device using secure local storage. We do not operate external gameplay servers to store your game activity.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support free development and updates, PipDrop integrates trusted third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to serve voluntary rewarded video ads and occasional interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA), coarse usage data, and diagnostic metrics (crash reports and performance data) strictly to deliver personalized advertisements and prevent fraud, in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

PipDrop does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. All core puzzle gameplay functions entirely offline without transmission of personal data.
